We’re early in the journey. Here’s what we can tell you today.

What is Orbit?

Orbit is a proposed all-in-one website for everyday digital tasks. The aim is to bring useful text, image, file, and developer utilities into one consistent workspace.

Can I use the tools right now?

You can use the text demo on this page now. The dashboard is a concept preview; the wider platform and other tool categories are still in development.

Who is it for?

Anyone who regularly jumps between websites to complete small tasks—especially creators, students, developers, freelancers, and small teams.

Will Orbit be free?

A free tier is being considered, alongside a possible paid option for heavier use. Neither the plans nor their limits have been finalized.

What happens to text I enter in the demo?

The demo processes it locally in your browser. This implementation does not send the text to a server, use analytics, or save it in browser storage. Other tools will need their own documented data-handling policies before launch.

When will early access open?

A launch date hasn’t been announced. Once signup is enabled, the early-access list will be the place to hear about availability and opportunities to share feedback.
