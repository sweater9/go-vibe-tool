Contact & Feedback

Help us make the tools better.

Found a bug, have an idea, or want to suggest a tool? We want to hear it.

What to send

Useful feedback includes the tool name, what you expected to happen, what actually happened, and your browser/device if the issue appears technical. Please avoid sending passwords, private keys, financial credentials, sensitive personal information, or confidential material.

Suggest a tool

We are especially interested in repetitive digital tasks that currently require too many steps, websites or manual clean-up.

GitHub

You can review the project and share technical feedback through the IntelliTools GitHub repository.
