GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations before you use it.

Will it find every piece of personal data?

No. It uses patterns for common formats. Names, addresses and unusual formats can be missed, so read the text yourself as well.

Where is my data processed?

In your browser, on your device. This tool has no server-side component, so what you enter is not uploaded by GoVibeTool, and no account is needed.
