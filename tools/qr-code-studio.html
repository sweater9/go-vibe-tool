GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations before you use it.

Does a generated QR code stay dependent on IntelliTools?

No. A standard QR code contains the information you encoded; once saved, scanning it does not require the IntelliTools page.

Will every camera work with the QR reader?

Camera reading depends on browser permissions and device/browser support. You can use an image when camera access is unavailable.
