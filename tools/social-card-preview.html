GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations before you use it.

Are the previews exact?

No. They are approximations of each platform's layout. Nothing is fetched from your site or from the platforms, so always confirm with the platform's own validator before launch.

Where is my data processed?

In your browser, on your device. This tool has no server-side component, so what you enter is not uploaded by GoVibeTool, and no account is needed.
