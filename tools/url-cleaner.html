GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations before you use it.

Will a cleaned link still open the same page?

Usually yes. Common tracking parameters do not change the destination, but some sites use parameters for content, so open the cleaned link once to confirm.

Where is my data processed?

In your browser, on your device. This tool has no server-side component, so what you enter is not uploaded by GoVibeTool, and no account is needed.
