GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations before you use it.

Does this independently verify whether a claim is true?

No. It compares claims with the evidence you supply. It is not an independent fact-checking service or definitive hallucination detector.

What does “Not found” mean?

It means the checker did not find enough textual support in the supplied evidence under its matching rules; it does not by itself prove the claim is false.
