GOOD TO KNOW

Clear expectations before you use it.

Does the generator save my generated password to an IntelliTools account?

No IntelliTools account is required. The generator is designed to work in the browser rather than storing generated passwords in an account.

Does a strength indicator guarantee a password cannot be compromised?

No. A strength indicator is only a local estimate. Use unique credentials and an appropriate password manager for important accounts.
