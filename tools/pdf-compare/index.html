      What this compares, and what it does not
      • Compares the selectable text of the two PDFs, in the order it is stored. Both files are read in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
      • Scanned or image-only pages have no text to read. They are listed as not compared. There is no OCR.
      • Images, drawings, colours, fonts, layout, form fields, comments and links are not compared.
      • Text in columns, tables, headers and footers can be stored in an unusual order, which may show up as extra differences.
      • Moved text appears as one removal and one addition. Words split across lines with a hyphen are re-joined.
      • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first. Limits: 50 MB and 300 pages per file.
      • A "no differences" result means the readable text matched, not that the files are identical.
