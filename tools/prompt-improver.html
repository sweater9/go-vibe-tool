GOOD TO KNOW

Do I need an account?

No. You can open and use the tool without creating a GoVibeTool account.

Does the tool send my prompt to an AI model?

No. The analysis uses fixed rules that run in your browser. It checks the structure of your prompt, not facts, and it cannot predict how a particular model will answer.
