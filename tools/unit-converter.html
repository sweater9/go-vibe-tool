Are the currency rates live?

No. Currency conversion uses a dated rate snapshot stored with the tool, shown next to the result. Check a live source before making a payment.

Where is my data processed?

In your browser, on your device. This tool has no server-side component, so what you enter is not uploaded by GoVibeTool, and no account is needed.
