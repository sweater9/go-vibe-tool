Privacy

Privacy, without the mystery.

We design IntelliTools to minimize unnecessary collection of information.

Last updated: October 2026
Browser-first by design. The current launch tools are designed to perform their core processing in your browser where practical.

Information you enter into tools

For the current browser-first tools, inputs are processed locally in your browser for their core functionality rather than being sent to an IntelliTools application server. You do not need an IntelliTools account to use these launch tools.

Website infrastructure

The site is hosted using third-party web infrastructure. Like most websites, hosting and network providers may process technical request information needed to deliver the site, such as IP addresses, timestamps and browser request data, according to their own practices.

Analytics and advertising

IntelliTools uses Google AdSense to display advertising. When advertising resources load, Google and its partners may receive technical information about your visit and may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where applicable, personalization. This activity is separate from the processing of files and information within IntelliTools browser-based tools. Advertising is not required for the tools' core processing functions.

For details about Google's use of information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can manage available advertising and cookie preferences through your browser and applicable Google controls. Disabling advertising resources may affect which ads appear, but does not prevent core browser-side tool processing.

Local browser storage

Some features may use browser storage to remember settings or support functionality on your device. You can clear site data through your browser controls.

External links

Links to third-party websites are governed by those sites' own privacy practices.

Changes

As IntelliTools grows, this notice may change to reflect new features or services. The update date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Questions

For privacy questions or feedback, use our contact page.
