How to play

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. Each guess must be a real word. After each guess the tiles show how close you are:

Green — right letter, right spot. Yellow — letter is in the word, wrong spot. Grey — letter is not in the word (or you already used all of its copies). Repeated letters are counted carefully.

A new daily puzzle appears each day at local midnight and is the same for everyone. Practice gives unlimited extra words and does not affect your streak. Everything runs locally; progress and statistics stay on this device.
