2048 Challenge

Slide and merge tiles to reach 2048.

How to play

Use the arrow keys (or W A S D), swipe on the board, or tap the arrow buttons. Tiles with the same number merge into one when they collide. Each merge adds the new tile's value to your score. Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score. The game ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge. Your best score and current game are saved only on this device.
