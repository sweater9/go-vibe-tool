RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

techniquefreshness: evolvingsources not yet verified

RLHF trains a language model to behave in ways people prefer: gather human comparisons of outputs, fit a reward model, then optimise the model against that reward with reinforcement learning under a KL constraint.

Short answer: RLHF = SFT → reward model from human preference comparisons → RL (typically PPO) with a KL penalty to the SFT model. It made instruction-following chat models practical but is complex, can be gamed, and can encourage sycophancy.

What is it?

After basic training, people compare pairs of the model's answers and say which is better. A "reward model" learns to predict those preferences, and the AI is then trained to produce answers the reward model scores highly — while being kept from drifting too far from its original behaviour.

Also known as: RLHF, reinforcement learning from human feedback, reward model training, human feedback training, InstructGPT method, reward model, PPO RLHF, how ChatGPT was trained.

Technical definition

Pipeline popularised by Christiano et al. (2017) and applied to language models in summarisation work (Stiennon et al., 2020) and InstructGPT (Ouyang et al., 2022). Components: preference data collection, reward model training (Bradley–Terry loss), RL optimisation (PPO) with a KL penalty against a reference policy to limit over-optimisation. Known limitations catalogued by Casper et al. (2023): evaluator error and bias, reward misspecification, reward hacking (Reward Hacking and Over-Optimization), mode collapse and sycophancy (Sycophancy in Language Models). Alternatives and complements: Preference Optimization (Aligning Models from Human or AI Preferences) methods such as Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), and AI feedback (Constitutional AI and RLAIF (AI Feedback)). RLHF uses RL as a component but is typically classified as an alignment technique.

Entity type: technique · Freshness class: evolving.

When should I use it?

Mostly the concern of model developers; consumers should understand its limitations.

Prerequisites and related concepts

Understand these first: Instruction Tuning, Reinforcement Learning, Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO).

Related concepts: Preference Optimization (Aligning Models from Human or AI Preferences), Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), DPO vs RLHF, Reward Hacking and Over-Optimization, Sycophancy in Language Models, Constitutional AI and RLAIF (AI Feedback), AI Alignment, Human Preference Evaluation (Arenas and Elo Rankings).

Implementation patterns

  • Train the reward model on diverse, well-guided comparisons.
  • Keep a KL penalty; monitor reward vs held-out human eval.
  • Refresh the reward model as the policy improves.
  • Red-team the policy for reward-model exploits.

Technologies and frameworks

Commonly associated: TRL, OpenRLHF, DeepSpeed-Chat, reward-model datasets. Names are examples of what exists, not recommendations, and projects change quickly.

Practical guide

  1. Write annotation guidelines.
  2. Collect comparisons.
  3. Train and validate the reward model.
  4. Run PPO with KL control.
  5. Evaluate with humans and held-out sets.

Common failure modes

  • Reward hacking.
  • Annotator disagreement and bias.
  • Over-optimisation reducing quality.
  • Heavy compute and engineering.
  • Sycophancy and verbosity.

Troubleshooting

  • Reward rises but human ratings fall — Increase KL, update the reward model with fresh comparisons, early-stop on human eval.
  • Model flatters users — Add disagreement-appropriate pairs and sycophancy evals.

Contrasted with

  • Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) — DPO skips the reward model and RL loop.
  • Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning (RLVR and GRPO) — RLVR uses programmatic verifiable rewards, not human preference models.
