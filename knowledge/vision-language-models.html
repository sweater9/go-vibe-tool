Vision-Language Models (VLMs)

conceptfreshness: evolvingsources not yet verified

Vision-language models take images (and often video) together with text and answer questions, describe scenes, read documents and follow visual instructions.

Short answer: A VLM pairs a vision encoder with a language model via a connector, so it can reason over images and text together. Strengths: description, OCR-like reading, chart/screenshot Q&A. Weaknesses: fine spatial detail, counting, and confident hallucination about image content.

What is it?

A vision-language model can look at a picture and talk about it: describe it, answer questions about a chart, read text in a screenshot. Under the hood it converts the image into a form the language model can read alongside your words.

Also known as: VLM, VLMs, multimodal LLM, MLLM, image understanding model, LLaVA, vision language model, image to text model.

Technical definition

Common architecture: a pre-trained image encoder (ViT/CLIP-style, Vision Transformers (ViT)) produces visual tokens; a projector or cross-attention maps them into the LLM's embedding space; the LLM generates text conditioned on both (Flamingo, Alayrac et al., 2022; BLIP-2, Li et al., 2023; LLaVA, Liu et al., 2023). Training typically involves image–text alignment then instruction tuning on visual instructions. Known issues: object hallucination, resolution limits, counting and spatial reasoning errors, and sensitivity to image crop/quality.

Entity type: concept · Freshness class: evolving.

When should I use it?

Use for image/screenshot understanding, document and chart Q&A, accessibility description.

Prerequisites and related concepts

Understand these first: What is Multimodal AI?, Vision Transformers (ViT), What is a Large Language Model (LLM)?.

Related concepts: Contrastive Learning and CLIP, Document Understanding AI (OCR, Layout, Extraction), Computer-Use and Browser Agents, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models, How to Evaluate AI Systems and Outputs.

Implementation patterns

  • Send images at adequate resolution and crop regions of interest.
  • Ask for structured outputs and verify extracted values.
  • Combine OCR with a VLM for dense text.
  • Evaluate on your own image set.

Technologies and frameworks

Commonly associated: LLaVA family, BLIP-2, Hugging Face Transformers, provider multimodal APIs. Names are examples of what exists, not recommendations, and projects change quickly.

Practical guide

  1. Define the visual task.
  2. Test candidate models on 30–50 real images.
  3. Optimise image size/cropping.
  4. Validate outputs with rules or humans.
  5. Review privacy (AI Privacy and Security: What to Know Before You Paste Data).

Common failure modes

  • Object hallucination.
  • Misreading small text or dense tables.
  • Counting errors.
  • Sending sensitive images to third parties.
  • Prompt injection through text embedded in images.

Troubleshooting

  • Wrong numbers read from a chart — Provide higher resolution or crop; cross-check with data when available.
  • Invents details — Ask for what is visibly present only; require uncertainty statements; verify.

Contrasted with

  • Contrastive Learning and CLIP — CLIP embeds images and text for matching; VLMs generate text about images.
  • Document Understanding AI (OCR, Layout, Extraction) — Document AI often adds OCR and layout steps that a general VLM may handle implicitly.
